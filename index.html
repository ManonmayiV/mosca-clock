How the scoring works

  • Critical: key exchange is quantum-vulnerable and y > z. Traffic recorded today will still be secret when a CRQC exists ("harvest now, decrypt later").
  • High: key exchange is quantum-vulnerable and x + y > z. The Mosca inequality fails.
  • Medium: within a 2-year margin of failing, or signatures stay classical past the CRQC year (x > z).
  • Low: vulnerable crypto, but the timeline leaves room.
  • Ready: everything recorded is quantum-safe.

Priority = status weight × sensitivity × 1.5 if internet-facing, plus years of exposure. Signatures cannot be harvested; they only matter once a CRQC exists. Byte sizes come from FIPS 203, 204 and 205. Everything runs in your browser and is saved only to this browser's local storage.
